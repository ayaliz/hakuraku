# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 April 2026

Hakuraku is a fan-made website for race analysis, replay viewing, and related Uma Musume data tools.

Hakuraku is operated as a non-commercial fan project.

This page explains what data Hakuraku processes, why it is processed, and what choices you have.

If you have any privacy-related questions, contact `privacy@hakuraku.moe`.  
For general questions, contact `contact@hakuraku.moe`.

## Accounts

If you create a Hakuraku account, we store:

- your account name;
- your email address;
- a hashed version of your password;
- account verification and password reset token records;
- session records used to keep you signed in.

Your password is not stored in plain text.

We use this data to:

- create and manage your account;
- verify your email address;
- let you sign in securely;
- let you reset your password;
- let you delete your account.

## Race and replay data

Hakuraku processes race archive data to power replay viewing, filtering, analytics, and search features.

Depending on the source race file, this can include:

- race IDs and replay/archive IDs;
- character, card, support card, skill, strategy, and result data;
- race timings, placements, and team compositions;
- in-archive trainer names and in-game viewer IDs.

Public replay and race pages are designed so that raw archive files are not exposed directly in the browser by default.

Where possible, replay payloads sent to the browser are sanitized. Some source fields may still be stored on the backend where they are needed for indexing, account linking, race history, or abuse prevention.

## Optional game account linking and race history

Hakuraku offers an optional feature that lets you link your in-game Uma Musume account to your Hakuraku account.

If you use this feature, Hakuraku processes:

- your in-game viewer ID;
- your public in-game trainer name;
- the link between your Hakuraku account and your verified game account;
- horseACT verification records and short-lived or reusable API credentials issued for that link;
- a trained character roster snapshot sent from horseACT;
- matching race records already present in Hakuraku's database that are associated with that viewer ID.

Hakuraku uses this data to:

- verify that the in-game account belongs to the Hakuraku user performing the link;
- show your stored veteran roster on your account page;
- show your linked race history from indexed races already present in Hakuraku datasets;
- let you load that linked race history into multi-race analysis.

This feature is optional. If you do not use it, Hakuraku does not link a game account to your Hakuraku account.

## Emails

If you register an account, request a password reset, or contact us by email, we process the email data needed to send and receive those messages.

Account emails such as verification and password reset emails are sent through third-party email infrastructure.

## Technical and security data

Like most websites, Hakuraku also processes technical data automatically when you use the site. This may include:

- IP address;
- request metadata;
- browser and device information;
- session and cookie data needed for login and security;
- rate limiting, logging, and anti-abuse data.

We use this data to keep the site working, secure accounts, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot problems.

## Service providers

Hakuraku uses third-party infrastructure providers to run the service. At the time of writing, these include:

- Cloudflare, for site delivery, Workers execution, caching, and storage access;
- Hetzner, for PostgreSQL hosting;
- Resend, for account-related transactional email.

These providers may process data on our behalf as part of providing their infrastructure or email services.

## Why data is processed

Hakuraku processes data to:

- provide the website and account system;
- power replay viewing, search, indexing, and analytics;
- support optional account-linked race history features;
- send verification and password reset emails;
- prevent abuse and secure the service;
- respond to emails and support requests.

## Legal basis

If you are in a jurisdiction that requires a legal basis for processing, such as the GDPR, Hakuraku generally relies on:

- performance of a contract, where processing is needed to provide your account and related features you asked to use;
- legitimate interests, where processing is needed to operate the site, provide analytics and replay tools, keep the service secure, and prevent abuse;
- legal obligations, where processing is required by applicable law.

## Cookies

Hakuraku uses only the cookies and browser storage needed for core site behavior, such as keeping users signed in and protecting account sessions.

This currently includes a first-party session cookie used for login and account authentication.

At the time of writing, Hakuraku does not use advertising or marketing cookies.

Hakuraku may also rely on strictly necessary security or infrastructure cookies set by service providers such as Cloudflare when needed to deliver and protect the site.

## Data sharing

Hakuraku does not sell personal data.

Data may be shared with service providers that help operate the site, or where necessary to comply with legal obligations, enforce site rules, or protect the service and its users.

## International transfers

Hakuraku uses infrastructure and email providers that may process data in countries outside your own, including the European Union and the United States.

Hakuraku currently uses:

- Hetzner infrastructure is hosted in Germany;
- Cloudflare and Resend may process data through infrastructure that includes the United States and other regions.

We use established providers because they offer security, reliability, and data protection measures appropriate for the service they provide.

## Data retention

Data is kept only for as long as it is needed for the service, security, and legitimate operational purposes.

In practice:

- account data is kept while your account is active;
- verification, reset, and session records expire or are deleted when no longer needed;
- linked game-account records, horseACT verification records, and stored veteran snapshots are kept while the related account link remains active;
- race archive and replay index data may be retained as part of the site archive and analytics database;
- if you delete your account, account data associated with that account, including linked game-account records, horseACT verification records, veteran snapshots, active sessions, and account-owned shared uploads, is removed from the live account system, subject to any limited retention needed for security or legal reasons.

## Your choices and rights

If you have an account, you can:

- delete your account;
- request a password reset;
- contact us about your data.

If you have privacy-related questions or requests, email `privacy@hakuraku.moe`.

If you are in a jurisdiction that gives you legal rights over your personal data, such as the GDPR, you can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

## Children

Hakuraku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact `privacy@hakuraku.moe` and we will review the request.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If it changes, the updated version will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
